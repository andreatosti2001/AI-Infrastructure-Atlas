## Part 1 — The eight questions (PEF §4)

### 1. What was intended

The roadmap's M3, three sessions:

- **S11:** "one complete user journey from source → entity → relationship → visualization → evidence".
  Gate: "usable by a person who did not build the dataset". It must include "at least one editorial-quality
  analytical visual … connect[ed] visibly to the evidence chain".
- **S12:** "independently challenge the canonical data model and the first vertical slice" on ten named
  items, with "Architecture Audit Report + remediation decisions".
- **S13:** "test whether the content layer is stronger than the presentation layer". Gate: "No scaling until
  evidence weaknesses are corrected or explicitly documented".

### 2. What was implemented

| Session | Delivered | Record |
|---|---|---|
| S11 | the HBM journey page `site/hbm-chain/index.html`, generated by `tools/build_page.py` from `data/`, with an evidence panel on every mark and every unknown drawn as a named gap; page checks PG-1 to PG-10; browser QA outside CI | [S11 report](../../../sessions/reports/SESSION-11-REPORT.md); D-100 to D-108; `vertical-slice.md` |

**Against the gates:**

- **S11's gate is met in part.** The page was reviewed by the human, but "the findings are Claude's
  proposals, agreed, not the human's own walk-through" (S11 report, "Acceptance criterion 8 is met in part").
- **S13's gate is met.** Every weakness was corrected (F-1) or documented with an owner (D-113).

### 3. What changed, and why

- **The refused candidates moved from a Markdown table into records** (S12, D-109). The page had parsed
  the table twice and resolved records by display name.
- **Values about named records belong in `data/`, rules in documents** (D-110). Four more document tables
  holding per-record values became debt DT-1, done in S14 (D-117).
- **How human decisions are recorded changed** (D-114): outcomes and dates, not verbatim answers. S13's
  files were rewritten that way the same day.
- **The page grew a second consumer of its rules:** `source-policy.md`, `relationship-taxonomy.md` and
  `company-dataset.md` became page inputs (S11, Deviation 4). Any change to them now requires a rebuild.

### 4. Which assumptions failed

| Assumption | What happened | Record |
|---|---|---|
| S10's three navigation outputs would serve the page | the journey also needed actors and an `instance_of` walk | S11, invalidated assumptions |
| "SK hynix's M16" belongs on the HBM chain | no record links it to HBM; it became a separate lane | S11; D-100, D-104 |
| A word guard keeps refused relations out | a probe adding the refused "NVIDIA designs H100" failed no data check; only reading refused it | S12, audit S10-1 |
| The weight-bearing set is six claims | it is 14, because an input has an input of its own | S13, invalidated assumptions |
| A byte hash signals change on a web page | three hashes in one evening, same text | S13, process lessons |
| Claims are where the weaknesses are | the claims held; a refused-candidate record had narrowed its own quote (F-1) | S13, process lessons |

### 5. Which shortcuts were taken

- **The new-user test was replaced by Claude's proposals,** agreed by the human (S11). The project's own
  gate for S11, a person who did not build it, was not tested.
- **A checkpoint with known failures was pushed** to satisfy the environment's stop hook (S12, Deviation 1;
  CI run 103 failed). Nothing reached `main`.
- **Only the 20 sampled sources were re-retrieved** for byte changes (S13, Deviation 3).
- **Session harnesses live in `sessions/reports/`,** not `tools/`, with network bytes outside the
  repository (S12, S13). They are reproducible scripts, but not tested code.
- **No check was added for F-1,** because telling which words of a sentence are firms needs a reader
  (S13, Deviation 5).

### 6. What technical and research debt was created

- **S11:**
  - the page is stale whenever an input changes;
  - the journey specification is hand-chosen by ID;
  - the layout is fixed for one or two products;
  - browser QA is outside CI (S11, debt).
- **S12:** DT-1 to DT-11 (`M2-audit.md` Part 4), a new record kind to maintain, and `cand-NNN` counter IDs.
- **S13:** DT-S13-1 to DT-S13-10, each with an owner (D-113). The ones that reached M4 and later:
  - F-3, the H100 figures from a "preliminary" launch blog;
  - F-5, evidence ageing past the horizon unnoticed, now partly the source-age metric;
  - F-2, "party" standing unchecked on 14 citations;
  - F-8, the "HBM requires 3D die stacking" inference restates a definition.

### 7. What was removed or rejected

- **Visual forms:**
  - a table as the primary visual (kept as the text equivalent);
  - a force-directed network;
  - a placeholder "unknown supplier" node;
  - M16 drawn next to HBM (D-103, D-104).
- **Build options:** run-time JSON, a charting library, JavaScript, a commit stamp (D-102).
- **Refused relations as edges** in `relationships.json` (D-109).
- **A Samsung company record** (no Samsung filing read, S13).
- **A clock-reading freshness test** (S13): CI would turn red on a date, not on a change.

### 8. What should be carried into the next phase

All of it was carried into M4:

- the evidence-panel rule (every mark opens its evidence);
- the text-provenance rule (PG-1);
- gaps drawn with their state, never a zero;
- the D-114 to D-116 rules.

S14 owned DT-1, and S14 and S15 owned the access-date weakness.
