## 09 — Constraints

- **Evidence-first** (`CLAUDE.md` §5):
  - every supplier value rests on a retrieved, hashed source read at a locator, with an anchor quoted;
  - a search result is a lead, never a citation.
- **Claim discipline:**
  - a supplier's statement about its customer's product is a `FACT` from a party;
  - a statement by a third party is at most an `ATTRIBUTION`;
  - the Atlas's reasoning is a `DERIVATION`.
- **Unknown is never zero.** No research outcome turns an unknown into a zero, a placeholder or a
  silence.
- **Canonical data changes only on approval** (Class C, `CLAUDE.md` §9). Staging is where research
  lands.
- **Determinism:** rebuilt results and pages are byte-identical across runs.
- **Plain language first** (D-116, `CLAUDE.md` §9A) in every request to the human.
