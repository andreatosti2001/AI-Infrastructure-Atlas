## 06 — Required reasoning

**Before proposing (the rules):**

   - What makes a value "past": a `valid_to`, or an as-of date?
   - How does F-1 apply: not at all for a closed period, or to the evidence's date against the period?

## 09 — Constraints

- **Claim discipline:**
  - a teardown lab's observation is its own measurement;
  - a report of what another party said is an `ATTRIBUTION`;
  - the Atlas's reasoning is a `DERIVATION`.
- **Unknown is never zero.** A historical value never stands for the current state.
- **Determinism and plain language,** as in S16.5.
