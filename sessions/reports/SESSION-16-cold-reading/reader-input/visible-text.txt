Reading the chains

Each chain is one accelerator, read in the direction of its arrows: what acts on it, the accelerator, the part it incorporates with who makes that part beneath it, and what that part requires. The part and the process are the same records in every chain; they are repeated so that the chains line up and can be compared.

A solid line is stated by a source. A dashed line is the Atlas’s own inference. An amber dotted line or box is a gap, with its reason in words. A missing line means the Atlas has no record either way; it never means that no relationship exists. A date marked “access date” is only the day an undated web page was read. Every line opens its evidence.
