Atlas framing

Reading the chains

Each chain is one accelerator, read in the direction of its arrows: what acts on it, the accelerator, the part it incorporates with who makes that part beneath it, and what that part requires. The part and the process are the same records in every chain; they are repeated so that the chains line up and can be compared.

A solid line is stated by a source. A dashed line is the Atlas’s own inference. An amber dotted line or box is a gap, with its reason in words. A missing line means the Atlas has no record either way; it never means that no relationship exists. Every line opens its evidence.

Method and limitations

Atlas framing

What the counts count

The page is generated from the committed SQL result and the Atlas’s records by a tested build step; nothing on it is typed by hand. Its counts are tallies of the table’s rows, each shown beside the number of rows it is counted over. They describe what the Atlas records, never the industry, and a count of rows is not a count of records. The page computes no share, ranking or score, and no confidence value.
