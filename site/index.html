Method and limitations

Atlas framing

The page is generated from the Atlas’s data files by a tested build step; nothing on it is typed by hand. Only canonical records appear: records awaiting review are left out. The digest identifies the exact inputs; rebuilding from the same inputs gives the same page, byte for byte.

Rulings these pages depend on

  • D-190 The research journeys S20 builds
  • D-193 An entry page, linked from the built pages
