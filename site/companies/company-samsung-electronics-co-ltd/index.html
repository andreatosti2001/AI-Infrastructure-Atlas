Method and limitations

Atlas framing

Terms

Relation types

supplies
The source company sells or licenses the item (a product or a component class) to the target company, as a claim states at company level.

Claim types

ATTRIBUTION
What a named source says, defines, claims, plans or estimates, recorded as that source's statement. Superlatives, intentions and second-hand reports are always this type (ER-5, ER-6, ER-8).
DERIVATION
A proposition that follows from other claims, or from a recorded search, by a stated step. The Atlas's inference, shown as inferred.
FACT
A proposition about the world, stated by a first-hand source (standing party or originator) at its locator. Never the Atlas's own inference.

Evidence status

supported
The Verifier read each cited locator, found the anchor, and judged that the sentence states the proposition and the wording does not exceed it; for a claim with inputs, every input is supported and the stated step follows. MA §10 'direct', renamed.

Standing

originator
source-policy.md §4, row originator.
party
source-policy.md §4, row party.

Unknown and unstated values

not stated
The source gives no as-of date for this content.
undated
The source states no date of any kind (register: 'undated (live page)'). Its access date is its only temporal anchor.

Source classes

company filing
A disclosure a company makes to a regulator or exchange under a named legal reporting obligation (e.g. an annual report on Form 10-K or 20-F), for whose misstatement the company is legally accountable.
company technical documentation
Documentation in which a company specifies its own product, service or process for the people who build on, integrate or operate it: datasheets, architecture papers, developer and service documentation, user guides.
market research consultancy
Analysis, estimates or advice sold by a commercial market-research or consulting firm, including trackers, forecasts and analysts' communications.
standard specification
A document issued by a standards body through its standards process under a standard designation (e.g. a JEDEC JESD or an ISO/IEC number), defining terms, interfaces or measurement methods.
